HA NOIVietnam’s capital is steeped in history. The Old Quarter’s 36 guild streets are the heart of the action. Make time to visit a few excellent museums and witness local life along the lakes. Bring the whole family along and encounter the best of Vietnam’s nature and culture together.HOI ANHoi An’s rice paddies and beaches are great for families, with top amusement parks and golf courses in easy reach. See the countryside and Ancient Town by bicycle or by boat. CAN THOVietnam’s Mekong Delta is full of fun things to see and do, from sampan rides to fruit orchards, garden home stays to floating markets. River cruises make it easy to get from place to place.PHU QUOCThe whole family is bound to love the beaches and water parks on Phu Quoc island.
